## About Me

- 👋 Hi, I'm Enzo, a **Mathematical Engineer & Master of Data Science student** at Universidad de Chile.
- 🔬 I work on the **mathematical foundations of machine learning**: studying deep learning architectures and generative models as rigorous mathematical objects rather than heuristics, drawing on stochastic calculus, optimal control, and probability.
- 🎯 I'm particularly interested in **generative models from their probabilistic structure** (SDEs, score functions, Fokker–Planck) and **neural networks as continuous-time dynamical systems**.
- 🌏 I enjoy learning languages, and I'm currently learning Chinese **学无止境**.
